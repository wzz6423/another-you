# Code of conduct

**English** | [简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Shared expectations

Keep Another You's development spaces welcoming and professional. Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, or experience. Discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking the person behind them.

Constructive participation means giving specific feedback, accepting correction, respecting others' time, and taking responsibility for mistakes. Disagreement is welcome when it helps the project and remains respectful.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing someone else's private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not pressure contributors to share credentials, private conversations, or personal data.

## Scope and moderation

These expectations apply to project Issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces used for collaboration, including when someone officially represents the project.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove inappropriate content, or restrict participation according to the severity and repetition of the conduct. Moderation should explain the relevant behavior and protect the privacy of the people involved.

## Report a concern

Use an existing private channel to contact the maintainer. If no private route is available, request one without posting the details or identifying information publicly. Include the relevant context and links or other evidence that you can share appropriately.

For a software vulnerability, follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). Contribution workflow is described in [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Reference

These expectations draw on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), adapted to this project's current collaboration channels.
